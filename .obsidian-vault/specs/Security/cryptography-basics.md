## Definition

Cryptography Basics are the fundamental principles of encryption, hashing, and key management used to secure data in transit and at rest.

## Problem Addressed

Without Cryptography Basics:
- Data is exposed
- Secrets are insecure
- Compliance is at risk
- Users are at risk

## Scope of Boundaries

Cryptography Basics applies to:
- Encryption
- Hashing
- Symmetric/Asymmetric
- Key management

Cryptography Basics does not apply to:
- Backend code
- Database design
- Infrastructure
- Testing

## When to Apply

- Securing data
- Needing encryption
- Wanting hashing
- Managing keys

## Tradeoffs and When to Avoid

- **Complexity**: Cryptography Basics adds complexity
- **Not for simple apps**: Simple apps may not need Cryptography Basics
- **Not for internal**: Internal apps may not need Cryptography Basics
- **Not for all**: Not all apps need Cryptography Basics
